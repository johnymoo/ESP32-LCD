Fault injection: unreachable status endpoint, GB10-2, 2026-09-16.

Method: rebuild with CLUSTER_STATUS_URL pointed at tcp/9908 (no listener),
flash, capture 30 s, then restore the production URL, rebuild and reflash.
Both fans were unplugged, so "full speed" is the commanded PWM state only.

Relevant serial lines:

W (2684) fan_control: fault temp-invalid, fans forced to full speed
W (2714) cluster_display: status fetch failed: ESP_ERR_HTTP_CONNECT
W (4734) cluster_display: status fetch failed: ESP_ERR_HTTP_CONNECT
(further identical failures every ~2 s through the window; no crash)

After restoring the production URL the device booted, cleared the fault on
the first valid sample, and resumed the auto curve (LOW at ctrl 47 C); see
20260916-fan-control-boot.txt. The immediate per-sample fail path is covered
here; the 10 s staleness watchdog in fan_control recompute_fault() reuses the
same forced-full-speed branch and was verified by code review.
