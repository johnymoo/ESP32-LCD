poll stays at 2 s and tolerates the slower effective cadence.

Addendum (same day, after the first rollout)
--------------------------------------------
- User video showed boxes on every composed line: the middle dot U+00B7 is
  outside the built-in Montserrat charset. Replaced with the shipped bullet
  glyph (078a158); carousel slowed to ten seconds across agent, unit file,
  mirror, and firmware default.
- User video confirmed the SYSTEM six-bar layout clipped the CPU row and hid
  the footer; hero dropped to the 20 px font with a 13 px bar pitch and the
  panel extended two pixels (3ab09d7). GPU temperature side label shortened
  to a bare degree sign.
- MODEL page redesigned into THROUGHPUT / LATENCY / EFFICIENCY zones per the
  approved model-v2-1x mockup; queue/preempt chips colour under pressure and
  KV cache renders as an amber pressure bar (d1c8695).
- STOP retest completed on hardware: fans coast to ~300 RPM on a solid-low
  line and plateau; user accepted STOP = minimum-speed semantics, no
  supply-cut MOS (documented in FAN-CONTROL-20260916.md).
