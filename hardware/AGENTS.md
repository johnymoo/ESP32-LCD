# PCB scope and release boundaries

- PCB design and future revisions live here; DGX owns enclosure integration only.
- Current fabrication master is `fan-interface-revB/release-20260909-dfm5`.
  Preserve submitted Gerber, EPRO2, original BOM and CPL bytes. New designs get a new revision.
- Rev C is deferred under https://github.com/johnymoo/ESP32-LCD/issues/1 until
  Rev B hand-soldering results and user direction justify starting it.
- For Rev B buy the original full BOM as loose parts; no SMT and no substitutions
  to larger packages designed for Rev C. Round retail quantities to MOQ/pack multiples.
- PCB order review, final production artwork, assembly quality and electrical
  validation are separate facts. Do not call all of them passed after order review.
- Keep personal account, delivery, invoice and payment data out of this repository.
- Historical absolute paths are provenance. Local hardware checkout is under
  `/Users/chris/project/ESP32-LCD/hardware`; x570 backups remain under `~/project/PCB`.
